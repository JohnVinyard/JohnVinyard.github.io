Stacked DHO Synth

Two layers of damped harmonic oscillators. Layer 1's raw output is routed, node-to-node, into layer 2's tension — an FM-like chain built from a pseudo-physics spring approximation instead of sinusoidal operators. The control strips below are ordered bottom-to-top along the signal flow: inject energy into layer 1 at the bottom, then layer 1's and layer 2's own strips above that — each split top (damping, quicker decay) and bottom (tension mod: press to raise tension/pitch, release to return to base, like deforming a spring). Press and hold to target the nearest node, drag to sweep between neighbors, or use multiple fingers on a touch device for several at once.
